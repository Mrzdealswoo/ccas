SEAMLESS GLOBAL STUDY PATHWAYS

Study in Sri Lanka, Graduate Globally

Why spend millions on direct overseas tuition? Complete your Foundation and UK Level 5 HND at CCAS Campus in Colombo or Weligama, then articulate directly into the final year (Top-Up) in the UK, Australia, or Malaysia.

01

CCAS Foundation / Level 4

Begin your academic journey after O/Ls or A/Ls. Gain foundational mastery in computer science, business, or psychology in a supportive campus environment.

Duration: 9-12 Months in Sri Lanka
02

UK Level 5 HND (Higher Diploma)

Certified by Ofqual-regulated British awarding bodies (Gatehouse / TQUK). Equivalent to the first 2 years of a full British Bachelor's Honours Degree.

Save up to 70% in foreign living costs
03

Final Year Degree Top-Up

Fly directly to partner universities in the UK, Australia, or Malaysia with credit transfer, OR complete your Bachelor's Honours right here in Sri Lanka!
